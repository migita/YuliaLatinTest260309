## Limits and editorial decisions

P3 bottom is cropped after “STA structure: Subject + Time + Action”. The visible pattern is tested; unseen lower examples or further grammar are not claimed. The 不是 note is clipped at the right, but 不是 and its pronunciation are visible. P1 handwritten “Kan = to..” is incomplete; 看 is tested in 看书/看电影/看电视, not treated as a reliable standalone translation. Pinyin spacing is normalised; 谁 shéi/shuí are accepted. Sunday formal/informal follows the sheet. 是 is glossed be (am/is/are); past be depends on context, not a different Chinese word. Questions use 什么, 谁 and 你呢, not an invented 吗 syllabus. Reading passages are original combinations of visible vocabulary/patterns, not transcribed unseen source text. Time-first variants are accepted as grammatical alternatives and identified as such.
